Your journal. Your choice.

WakeState stores your health journal locally and does not automatically upload it. This notice explains the current app; your device and hosting providers also have their own privacy practices.

What stays on your device

Check-ins, events, sleep entries, medication records, and preferences are saved in local app or browser storage. No account is required. The app includes no advertising or analytics SDKs. WakeState does not provide its own encryption or app lock. Someone with access to your unlocked device or browser profile may be able to read your journal.

Optional feedback

If you open feedback, the app contacts Supabase to request an abuse-prevention challenge. Submitting sends your selected role, app section, feedback category, and any text you enter. Your journal is not attached. Network requests expose your IP address and request metadata to the service; providers may keep operational logs. Do not include names, medication details, or other sensitive health information in feedback.

Exports and sharing

Exports contain unencrypted health information. You choose where to save or share them. Receiving apps and people control the copies you give them. Native exports leave a temporary copy in the app cache until your next export, Clear Data, or device cache cleanup.

Deletion and backups

Settings → Clear All Tracking Data removes this app's local journal and export cache, while keeping preferences. It cannot remove exported copies or feedback already sent. Clearing browser data, uninstalling, or changing devices can remove local records. Save regular backups somewhere private. Device-level backup or transfer behavior depends on the platform; do not rely on it as your journal backup.

External services

The web host receives normal page-request metadata. External resource links contact their destination when opened. Fonts are bundled with WakeState and do not require requests to a font provider.

Personal tracking

WakeState is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent a medical condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical decisions.

For questions about the app, use About → Give Feedback. Do not include private health information.
